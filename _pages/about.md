**Junteng Liu**
*Ph.D. Candidate, Hong Kong University of Science and Technology*

## About Me

I am a first-year Ph.D. candidate in the [HKUST NLP Group](https://nlp.ust.hk/) at the [Hong Kong University of Science and Technology](https://www.ust.hk/), advised by [Professor Junxian He](https://www.junxianhe.com/). I graduated from [Shanghai Jiao Tong University](https://www.sjtu.edu.cn/) in June 2024. My research focuses on natural language processing and machine learning.

I was previously advised by Professor Junxian He during my undergraduate studies at SJTU.

## Research Interests

- **LLM Reasoning and Reinforcement Learning**
- **Hallucination in Vision-Language Models (VLM)**
- **LLM Truthfulness and Interpretability**

Junteng Liu is a first-year Ph.D. candidate at the 
[HKUST NLP Group](https://nlp.cse.ust.hk/), 
Computer Science and Engineering Department, Hong Kong University of Science and 
Technology. He is supervised by [Prof. Junxian He](https://nlp.cse.ust.hk/people/junxian/).

Prior to joining HKUST, Junteng received his B.Eng. from 
[Shanghai Jiao Tong University](https://www.sjtu.edu.cn/) in 2024, where he was 
among the recipients of the Zhiyuan Honor Scholarship.

## Research Experience

**Ph.D. Candidate** | HKUST NLP Group | 2024 – Present
* Research on natural language processing and machine learning
* Focus areas: LLM reasoning, reinforcement learning for LLMs, hallucination in vision-language models (VLMs), LLM truthfulness and interpretability
* Supervised by Prof. Junxian He

**Research Intern** | [MINIMAX](https://www.max.covert.io/) | Feb 2025 – Present
* Working on reasoning models and RLHF for LLMs

**Research Intern** | Tencent WXG | Jun 2024 – Sep 2024
* Project: Chart understanding and hallucination mitigation in VLMs
* Mentored by Zifei Shan

**Research Intern** | Shanghai AI Lab | Jun 2023 – Dec 2023
* Project: Foundational research on Chinese foundation models
* Mentored by Prof. Yu Cheng

## Research Interests

* **LLM Reasoning and Reinforcement Learning** – Enhancing reasoning capabilities of large language models through scalable training techniques
* **Hallucination in Vision-Language Models** – Understanding and mitigating perception bottlenecks and hallucination in multimodal learning
* **LLM Truthfulness and Interpretability** – Studying the internal representations that govern truthful behavior in language models

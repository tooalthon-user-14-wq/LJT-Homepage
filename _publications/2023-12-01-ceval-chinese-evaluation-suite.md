We introduce **C-Eval**, a comprehensive, multi-level, multi-discipline evaluation suite for foundation models in the Chinese language. The benchmark covers 57 subjects across four difficulty levels, spanning humanities, social sciences, STEM, and practical skills.

C-Eval addresses the gap in Chinese-specific benchmarking with the following innovations:

* **Multi-Level Coverage**: The evaluation spans from high school (Level 1) to professional/doctoral (Level 4) proficiency, enabling comprehensive assessment across education levels.
* **Multi-Discipline Design**: 57 diverse subjects ranging from political science to software engineering to clinical medicine.
* **Open-Generated Evaluation Protocols**: All evaluation instances are generated through a pipeline combining syllabus extraction, question generation, and tuning.
* **Standardized Evaluation Framework**: Setting a community standard for Chinese benchmarking.

C-Eval has been widely adopted and is referenced in over 500 papers as a community standard for evaluating Chinese foundation models.
